# Third-Party Risk Management

## Project Overview

The Third-Party Risk Management project aims to identify, evaluate, and manage risks arising from vendors, suppliers, and partners. This framework helps ensure that third-party relationships are assessed, monitored, and governed effectively, contributing to the overall stability and security of the organization.

## Objectives

- Identify third-party risks affecting the organization.
- Assess the impact and likelihood of vendor and partner risks.
- Develop and implement mitigation and oversight strategies.
- Monitor and review third-party risks and controls regularly.

## Deliverables

- **Third-Party Risk Assessment Report**: Document detailing identified third-party risks, their impact, and likelihood.
- **Vendor Risk Register**: A centralized repository of all vendors, their risk ratings, and mitigation plans.
- **Due Diligence Checklist**: Structured checklist for onboarding and periodic review of third parties.
- **Third-Party Risk Management Plan**: Comprehensive plan outlining the process for managing and monitoring third-party risks.

## Project Phases

### 1. Vendor Identification and Onboarding

- **Objective**: Identify all third parties and bring them into the risk program.
- **Activities**:
  - Inventory vendors, suppliers, and partners.
  - Classify third parties by criticality and data access.
  - Collect due diligence documentation at onboarding.

### 2. Risk Assessment

- **Objective**: Evaluate the impact and likelihood of third-party risks.
- **Activities**:
  - Perform risk assessments on vendors by tier.
  - Review security questionnaires, certifications, and attestations.
  - Prioritize vendors based on risk severity and business criticality.

### 3. Mitigation Planning

- **Objective**: Develop strategies to mitigate third-party risks.
- **Activities**:
  - Define contractual security and compliance requirements.
  - Develop remediation plans for high-risk vendors.
  - Assign responsibilities for vendor oversight.

### 4. Implementation

- **Objective**: Implement third-party risk controls and oversight.
- **Activities**:
  - Execute remediation plans with vendors.
  - Monitor the progress and effectiveness of mitigation measures.
  - Adjust strategies as needed based on vendor performance.

### 5. Monitoring and Review

- **Objective**: Continuously monitor and review third-party risks.
- **Activities**:
  - Regularly update the vendor risk register.
  - Conduct periodic reassessments and audits of key vendors.
  - Communicate third-party risk status to stakeholders.
